Who we are

Fettle is a boutique home-care service house for a small, hand-picked roster of London homeowners. We sit deliberately apart from the one-man-band handyman and the anonymous marketplace — fewer clients, better work, every job carrying a name and a reputation.

A small permanent team of multi-skilled generalists does the day-to-day work. A short bench of personally-vetted specialists covers the licensed trades — plumbing, rendering, tree surgery — so there is always a proper hand for the job.

How we work

From a wonky fence to a fresh coat of paint, one team does the lot — painting, plastering, plumbing, fences, floors, odd jobs. The same faces each visit. People who know where the stopcock is, and which floorboard creaks.

Dust sheets down, shoes off, radio low. Work you notice only by the fact everything simply works.
