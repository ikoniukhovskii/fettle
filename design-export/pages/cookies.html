Cookies

A short site with a light touch. We use only what keeps it working — nothing that follows you around.

Last updated 2 July 2026.

What we use

Fettle’s site uses a small number of essential cookies and local storage — the sort that remember your place on the page and keep the site running. Nothing more is needed to browse or to check availability.

What we don’t use

  • No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
  • No selling of browsing data to anyone.
  • No profiles built to follow you across other sites.

Analytics

If we ever add simple, privacy-minded analytics to understand which pages help, it’ll be counted anonymously and never tied to you by name.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. The essential ones only keep the site tidy, so blocking them may make a few things behave oddly.
