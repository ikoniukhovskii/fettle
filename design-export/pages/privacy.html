Privacy

We keep as little as we need to do good work, and we don’t sell it on. Here’s the plain version.

Last updated 2 July 2026.

What we collect

When you check availability or get in touch, we take your postcode, your name, and a way to reach you — phone, WhatsApp, or email. When we visit, we note what needs doing at your home so we can price and plan the work.

Why we hold it

  • To tell you whether we have room on the list near you.
  • To arrange a first visit and quote the work.
  • To keep a record of jobs done at your home, so the next visit picks up where the last left off.

Who sees it

Our own team, and — only when a job needs a licensed trade — the vetted specialist doing that particular work. We don’t sell your details, and we don’t pass them to marketplaces or advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as you’re on our list, plus the period we’re required to keep records for tax and insurance. Ask us to delete what isn’t needed and we will.

Your say

You can ask to see what we hold, correct it, or have it removed. Write to hello@fettle.london and a person — not a bot — will sort it.

Questions about any of this? Get in touch.
